## Research pass — 2026-10-31 (his order: "research what they look like and make them off a little bit so they're not copyrighted")
Full report: ~/workspace/research_notes/orisha-thoth-iconography-copyright-20261031-1257/report.md
- **Copyright verdict:** the tradition itself is public heritage — names, domains, sacred colors, symbols, tools, traditional attire are NOT copyrighted (US Copyright Office: copyright never protects ideas, facts, familiar symbols). What IS protected is a specific modern artist's version (exact face, costume design, silhouette, logo). Rule going forward: draw ONLY from tradition + our own invented faces/details; never trace or closely copy any modern artwork, game, film, or comic design. Our new material is then copyrightable as ours. (Research, not legal advice; a USPTO trademark clearance search on names/logos is worth doing before any commercial launch — god names alone can't be monopolized, but stylized marks exist.)
- **Sensitivity law for designs:** these are living religions (Yoruba/Ifá, Lucumí, Candomblé). Eshu is a messenger/enforcer — NEVER a villain or devil. No caricatures, no consecrated objects used as mere decoration, no false claims of initiation/authenticity. Colors/numbers vary by lineage — we document which lineage each card follows.
- **Corrections/sharpening to the draft cards:**
  - Yemaja: seven skirts of blue and white is the traditional marker.
  - Obatala: silver staff (opaxoro) + white horsetail whisk (irukere); serene elder, confirmed.
  - Babalu-Aye: burlap is the constant (colors vary by path); sacred aja broom, two crutches, two dogs.
  - Osanyin: traditional form is one-eyed, one-armed, one-legged — sacred form, depict respectfully, never as a monster.
  - Ochosi: colors genuinely diverge by lineage (Lucumí blue + yellow; Candomblé Ketu blue; some houses green) — LINEAGE CHOICE still open for Lijah; current draft card uses forest green/ochre.
  - Thoth/Thootie: Kemetic tradition has NO single sacred color for Thoth — his palette is black-and-white ibis plumage + silver/white moonlight; our emerald/jade is a house design choice, labeled as such (never presented as ancient fact).
